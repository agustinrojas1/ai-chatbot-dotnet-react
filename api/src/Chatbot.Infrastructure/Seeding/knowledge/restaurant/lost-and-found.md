If you leave something behind at Luigi's, we keep it for a limited time so you can claim it. Items are kept at the host stand.

## What we keep

Items left in the dining room, on the patio or in the restrooms go into a labeled lost-and-found box at the host stand. We keep most items for 30 days. Valuables, such as phones, wallets and keys, are kept in the office behind the counter. We do not keep perishable food, and we cannot store items left in the parking lot.

## How to claim an item

Message us on WhatsApp or email help@luigispizzeria.example with a description of the item and the date of your visit. Include a photo if you have one. A team member replies during opening hours, and you can collect the item at the host stand from 12:00 to 22:00.

## Unclaimed items

After 30 days, unclaimed items are donated to a local charity. We do not hold items for longer, so please check as soon as you can.
