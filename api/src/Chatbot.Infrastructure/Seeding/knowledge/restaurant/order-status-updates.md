We keep you informed about your order through WhatsApp messages, so you do not need to call the restaurant to check on it.

## When we send updates

You receive a message when your order is confirmed, when the kitchen starts preparing it, and when a delivery driver leaves with it. For pickup orders, we send a message when the order is ready, which is usually 20 minutes after you place it.

## Delivery timing

Deliveries take 35 to 50 minutes from confirmation, and we work with 30-minute delivery windows. The message sent when the driver leaves includes the window in which we expect to arrive. If your order arrives late, see our late or wrong order refunds guide for the $6 credit rule.

## Questions about your order

Send your order number on WhatsApp, and a team member replies during opening hours. Please avoid sending the same question several times, because it slows down the reply. Messages sent after closing are answered the next day.
