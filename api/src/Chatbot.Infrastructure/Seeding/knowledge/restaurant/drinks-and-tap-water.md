Our drink menu is short and simple. We do not serve alcohol, so the drinks below are all non-alcoholic.

## Drinks on the menu

The main drink on the menu is Limonata, a sparkling Italian lemonade in a 330 ml bottle, for $4. It is made with lemon, sparkling water and sugar, with no dairy or eggs, and it is gluten-free. Our full beverage list is always shared on WhatsApp.

## Tap water

Tap water is free on request and is served in a glass with ice. We do not charge for refills of tap water, and children can ask for a cup at no cost. For a whole table, we can bring a jug of tap water at no charge.

## Bringing your own drinks

Guests may bring their own soft drinks to dine in. On the patio, we ask that outside drinks be served in plastic or paper cups rather than glass bottles.
