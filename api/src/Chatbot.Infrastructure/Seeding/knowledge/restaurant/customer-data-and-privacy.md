We collect only the information we need to take your order, deliver it and run our loyalty program. This page explains what we keep and how you can ask us to delete it.

## What we collect

We store your WhatsApp number, your name, your delivery addresses, your order history and any allergy notes you write on an order. We do not ask for your date of birth. We never store full card numbers, because card payments are processed by our payment provider.

## How long we keep it

Order history is kept for 24 months, so you can check past orders and loyalty points. Delivery addresses are deleted after 12 months without an order. We do not sell or share personal data with advertisers.

## Your requests

To ask for a copy of your data or to request deletion, email help@luigispizzeria.example with the WhatsApp number you use with us. We reply within 5 business days.
