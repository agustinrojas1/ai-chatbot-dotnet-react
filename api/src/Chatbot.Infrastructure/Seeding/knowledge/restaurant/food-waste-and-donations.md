We plan our daily prep carefully to reduce food waste. Food we cannot serve is handled with the same care as the food we sell.

## Daily prep

We prepare dough, sauce and vegetables based on the previous week's orders, and we adjust the prep each day. Fresh basil and vegetables are bought several times a week, so they are used within a few days.

## Donations

Unsold bread and prepared food that is still within safe temperature limits is donated to a local food bank every evening. We record each donation. We never donate food that has been out of refrigeration for more than 2 hours.

## Composting

Vegetable scraps and used coffee grounds from our Tiramisu prep are collected by a local farm every Monday for composting. The farm collects about 20 kg each week and sends us a monthly note with the total weight collected. Leftover dough is frozen and used for the first batch of the next day, so nothing goes unused.
