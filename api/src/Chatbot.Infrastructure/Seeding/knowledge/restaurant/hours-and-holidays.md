## Regular hours

We are open every day from 12:00 to 22:00, Chicago time. Delivery orders are accepted until 21:30 so they arrive before closing. Pickup orders are also accepted until 21:30.

## Holidays and closures

We close on December 25 and January 1. On Thanksgiving we open with reduced hours, from 12:00 to 16:00, and delivery service stops at 15:30.

We also close when there is an emergency in the building or in the kitchen, and we let customers know on WhatsApp as soon as possible.

## Schedule changes

We may extend our hours on special dates, such as Independence Day or the week before Christmas. We give advance notice through our social media and in the welcome message in the chat. If you have a question about a particular day, ask us before you leave home.
