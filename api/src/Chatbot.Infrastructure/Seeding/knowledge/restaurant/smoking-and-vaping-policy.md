Luigi's Pizzeria is a smoke-free and vape-free restaurant. The rule applies inside the building and on the patio, and it covers the area near our entrances.

## Inside and on the patio

Smoking and vaping are not allowed in the dining room, in the restrooms or on the patio. Guests who want to smoke or vape must stand at least 10 meters away from the building entrances and the side door.

## The area near the entrances

Please keep the area near the side door clear, since pickup orders are handed over there. Cigarette butts and vape waste should go in the bins placed at the front of the building, not on the ground.

## Enforcement

If a guest smokes or vapes inside, a host asks them politely to step outside. Repeated breaches may lead us to ask a guest to leave. Staff can also ask a guest to move away from the entrance if the smoke affects other customers.
