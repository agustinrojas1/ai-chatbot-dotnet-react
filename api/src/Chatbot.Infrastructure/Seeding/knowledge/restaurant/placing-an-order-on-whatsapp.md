You can place every order through our WhatsApp chat, for delivery or pickup. There is no app to download and no online account to create. Just message us during opening hours, from 12:00 to 22:00 Chicago time.

## Step by step

Start by telling us the items you want and how many of each. Then send your name and, for delivery, the full address with the apartment or unit number. We reply with a line-by-line total before anything is confirmed, including the delivery fee and any gluten-free crust surcharge.

Orders are accepted until 21:30 for both delivery and pickup, so the kitchen can finish before closing.

## Confirming and paying

Once you approve the total, we confirm the order and give you an order number. Keep that number, because you will need it to change or cancel the order or to ask about a delivery. You can pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or cash on delivery, as explained in our payments and tips guide.
