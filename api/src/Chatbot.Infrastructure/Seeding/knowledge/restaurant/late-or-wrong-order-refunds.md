## Late deliveries

If your delivery arrives more than 25 minutes after the end of its delivery window, you get a $6 credit on your next order. The credit is added to your account automatically once the order is closed, so you do not need to claim it.

Weather, road closures and high-demand evenings can cause delays. The credit still applies, because the delay is measured against the window we gave you.

## Wrong or missing items

Tell us the same evening about any wrong or missing item, with a photo if you can. For a wrong item, we refund its price to your original payment method within 7 business days. For cash on delivery, we credit the amount to your account instead.

We do not refund food that was eaten, and we cannot refund a dish that was changed by the customer after leaving the kitchen.

## How to ask for a refund

Email help@luigispizzeria.example with your order number, the problem and the photo. A team member replies during opening hours.
