## How pickup works

Pickup orders are ready 20 minutes after you place them. We hold a pickup order for 15 minutes after it is ready. After that, the food may need to be reheated, and we cannot guarantee its quality.

Collect your order at the side door facing the parking lot. Show the order number from your WhatsApp chat, and the team will hand over the bag.

## Pickup discount

Pickup orders get 10% off the food subtotal. The discount does not apply to tips and cannot be combined with other promotions.

There is no minimum order for pickup, so a single drink or one garlic bread is fine.

## Changes and cancellations

Pickup orders follow the same change and cancellation rules as delivery orders. If you are running late, message us before the ready time so we can keep your food hot.
