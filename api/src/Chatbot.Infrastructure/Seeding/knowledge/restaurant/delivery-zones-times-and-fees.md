## Delivery zone and minimum order

We deliver to addresses less than 5 km from the restaurant. If the address is farther away, we cannot accept the order, but we can suggest another address within the zone, such as an office or a friend's house.

The minimum order for delivery is $15, before taxes and tip. There is no minimum for pickup at the restaurant.

## Delivery fees

- Up to 2 km: $2.50.
- Between 2 km and 5 km: $4.75.
- Orders of $40 or more: free delivery, in either band.

Distance is measured from the restaurant to your address, not in a straight line.

## Times and delivery windows

A delivery takes 35 to 50 minutes from the moment you confirm the order. We work with 30-minute delivery windows and accept up to 4 orders per window, so during peak hours it is best to order in advance.
