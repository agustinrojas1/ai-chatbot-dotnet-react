If you prefer not to meet the driver, you can ask for contactless delivery when you place your order. Here is how it works and what to expect.

## How to ask

Add the note "contactless" to your order, along with a short instruction, such as "leave at the front gate" or "leave at the second-floor door". The driver sends you a WhatsApp photo of the order at the drop-off point.

## Waiting and calls

The driver waits up to 2 minutes at the drop-off point. If you do not appear, the driver calls you on WhatsApp and then leaves the order at the drop-off point as you requested.

## Payment and limits

Contactless drop-off is only available for orders paid by card, because cash on delivery has to be collected in person. Delivery fees and the $15 minimum order apply as usual, and contactless orders must still be within our 5 km delivery zone.
