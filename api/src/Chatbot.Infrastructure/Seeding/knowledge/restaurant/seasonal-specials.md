Beyond our regular menu, we sometimes offer seasonal specials made with produce at its best. Specials are announced in our WhatsApp chat and on our social media.

## When specials appear

We usually run one or two seasonal specials at a time, for between 3 and 6 weeks. Specials use fresh produce such as bell peppers, tomatoes and basil, which we buy several times a week. A special is available only while its ingredients last, and we announce when it sells out.

## Pricing and availability

Special prices are shown in the WhatsApp menu message, and they follow the same rules as regular items. The gluten-free (sin TACC) crust is +$2 on any pizza, and the delivery minimum and fees still apply. Specials are not available for catering orders unless we agree on them in advance.

## Suggestions

Tell us which specials you would like to see by sending a message on WhatsApp. We review suggestions every month, and the most requested ideas are considered for the next season.
