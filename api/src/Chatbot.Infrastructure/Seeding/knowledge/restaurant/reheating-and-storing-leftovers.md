## Storing leftovers

Store leftovers in a closed container within 2 hours of delivery. In the fridge they last up to 3 days. Separate the portions with baking paper so they do not stick together.

## Reheating in the oven

Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Place the pizza directly on the rack or on baking paper, and heat it for 8 minutes. This way the base becomes crispy again and the cheese melts evenly.

A microwave heats faster, but the crust turns soft. If you use one, put a paper towel under the portion so it does not get soggy.

## Freezing

You can freeze portions for up to one month. Thaw them in the fridge the night before, never at room temperature, and then reheat them in the oven as described in the previous step.
