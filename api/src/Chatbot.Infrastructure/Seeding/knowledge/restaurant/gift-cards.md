## Denominations

Gift cards come in $25, $50 or $100. You can buy them online or ask us on WhatsApp, and we can send the code by email to the person receiving the gift.

## Using a gift card

Enter the code at checkout for a pickup or delivery order, or give it to us on WhatsApp when you order. The balance covers food, drinks and delivery fees. Tips are not covered, so pay them separately.

If the order total is higher than the balance, pay the difference with any accepted method.

## Terms

Gift cards do not expire. They cannot be exchanged for cash, and they cannot be used to buy another gift card. A lost card can be replaced only if you have the purchase receipt, so keep it until the balance is used.
