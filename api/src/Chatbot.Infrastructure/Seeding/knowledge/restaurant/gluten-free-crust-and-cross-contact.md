## What the gluten-free (sin TACC) crust includes

Any pizza on our menu can be ordered with a gluten-free (sin TACC) crust for a +$2 surcharge. The dough is made with gluten-free flour and is pre-baked for 4 minutes before we add the sauce and toppings. This keeps it crispy on the outside and stops it from falling apart when you cut it.

## Is it baked separately?

No. Here is the honest answer: we use a shared oven. The gluten-free dough is baked in the same oven as the pizzas made with wheat flour, and it is stretched on the same work table. We do not have a dedicated oven, table or utensils. The gluten-free crust reduces gluten, but it does not guarantee the absence of traces.

If you have celiac disease or a severe sensitivity, we recommend that you do not order pizza. You can choose gluten-free dishes from the menu, such as the Caprese or the gluten-free Penne, which is cooked in its own pot. Even so, our kitchen is not gluten-free.

## What to know when you order

Tell us when you order if you need the gluten-free (sin TACC) crust. If you have a severe allergy, write it in the notes field so it is marked on the kitchen ticket. When in doubt, ask before you buy.
