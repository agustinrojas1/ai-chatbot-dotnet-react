Our kitchen can prepare large orders, but there are limits on how many pizzas we can make in one WhatsApp order and in one delivery window. These rules help us keep every pizza fresh.

## Pizzas per order

You can order up to 10 pizzas in a single WhatsApp order. For orders of more than 10 pizzas, the team confirms the details with you before the kitchen starts. For events of 15 people or more, we recommend our catering service.

## Delivery windows

Each 30-minute delivery window accepts up to 4 orders. A single large order still counts as one order in the window, so for very large orders we may ask you to choose a later time. The team tells you the expected delivery time before you confirm.

## Pickup limits

Pickup orders have the same limits as delivery orders. They are ready 20 minutes after you place them, so a large pickup may need a little more time. We tell you the ready time when you confirm.
