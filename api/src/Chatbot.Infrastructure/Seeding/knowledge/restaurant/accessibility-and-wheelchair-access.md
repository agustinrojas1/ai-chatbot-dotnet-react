Guests with mobility needs can dine with us and collect their orders without difficulty. Please tell the host when you arrive if you need any special help.

## Entrances and seating

The main entrance has a ramp, and the door opens to 90 cm (36 in). The dining room has two wheelchair spaces, and tables can be moved on request to make more room. The patio is reached from the side door, which has level access.

## Accessible restroom

We have one accessible restroom next to the dining room, with a grab bar and a door that opens 90 cm wide. It is open to guests during our regular hours, from 12:00 to 22:00.

## Pickup and service animals

Pickup at the side door is level, with no steps. Service animals are always welcome in the dining room, on the patio and in the restroom area, as the law requires. For pets that are not service animals, see our dog-friendly patio guide.
