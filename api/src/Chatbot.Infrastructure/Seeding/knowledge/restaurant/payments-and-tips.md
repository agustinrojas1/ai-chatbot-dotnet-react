## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay, for both pickup and delivery. For delivery orders you can also pay in cash when the order arrives. Drivers carry change for up to $30. If you plan to pay with a larger bill, tell us when you place your order.

We do not accept checks or installment payments.

## Tips

Tips are not included in the total. For delivery, we suggest 18% of the subtotal. The tip is paid to the driver and goes entirely to them. You can leave whatever tip you like, including none.

For pickup, you can leave a tip at the counter or add it when you pay by card.

## Receipts

We send your receipt by WhatsApp once the payment is confirmed. If you need an invoice in a company's name, request it with your order number within 5 days after delivery, and the team will send it to you by email.
