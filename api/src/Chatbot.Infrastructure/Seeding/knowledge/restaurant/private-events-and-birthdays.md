## The back room

Our back room seats up to 28 guests for private events such as office parties, showers and birthdays. It is available for dinner service only, and it comes with a minimum spend of $200 on food and drinks.

Decorations are welcome if they are removed by the end of the event. Please keep music at a level that does not disturb the dining room.

## How to book

Book at least 14 days ahead by WhatsApp. Send us the date, the start time, the number of guests and whether you want a set menu built from our pizzas and sides. We confirm within one business day.

For larger parties or events that need catering at another venue, ask about our catering service instead.

## Birthdays

Tell us it is a birthday when you book. We bring a candle and a slice of Tiramisu on the house for the guest of honor. If you want a cake from outside, let us know in advance so we can store it safely.
