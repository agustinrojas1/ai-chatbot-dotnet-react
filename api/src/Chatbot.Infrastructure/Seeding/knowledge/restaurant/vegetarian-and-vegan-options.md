## Vegetarian dishes

These dishes are vegetarian: Margherita, Veggie, Quattro Formaggi, Caprese Salad, Gluten-Free Penne Arrabbiata, Garlic Bread, Tiramisu and Limonata. Vegetarian means no meat or fish, not that every ingredient is free of animal products.

## Cheese and animal rennet

Quattro Formaggi uses gorgonzola, parmesan and fontina. Traditional parmesan and many gorgonzolas are made with animal rennet. If you avoid animal rennet, ask us before you order, because we cannot confirm the rennet source for every cheese.

## Vegan options

We do not offer vegan pizza and we do not have vegan cheese. The only vegan dishes on the menu are the Gluten-Free Penne Arrabbiata and the Limonata, which contain no dairy or eggs. Ask for the penne without any cheese on top.
