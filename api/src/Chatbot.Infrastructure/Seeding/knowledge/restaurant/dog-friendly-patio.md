## Dogs on the patio

Well-behaved dogs are welcome on our outdoor patio, which is open April to October. Dogs must stay on the floor and not on chairs or tables. They are not allowed in the dining room, except for service animals as the law requires.

## Limits and etiquette

We allow two dogs per table at most. Please bring your own leash, and clean up after your dog. We can bring a water bowl on request, and our staff will not feed your pet from the kitchen.

## Pickup and delivery

Dogs cannot ride with you inside the restaurant, but you can wait at the side door with your dog while you pick up your order. Delivery orders are not affected by the patio rules.
