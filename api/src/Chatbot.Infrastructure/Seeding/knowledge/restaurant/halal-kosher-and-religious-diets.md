Luigi's Pizzeria does not hold halal or kosher certification. This page explains what we can and cannot do for guests who follow religious dietary rules.

## Pork and certification

Our Prosciutto & Mushroom pizza uses prosciutto cotto, which is pork, so it is not suitable for guests who avoid pork. We do not use a separate kitchen or separate utensils for any religious diet. Please ask us before you order the Pepperoni pizza, because we cannot confirm the source of its meat.

## Dishes without pork

Vegetarian dishes that contain no pork include the Margherita ($12), the Veggie ($14), the Quattro Formaggi ($16), the Caprese Salad ($9) and the Gluten-Free Penne Arrabbiata ($13). Many guests who avoid pork choose these dishes. Our allergen guide lists pork only for the Prosciutto & Mushroom pizza, so please check the allergen guide for each dish you are considering.

## Cheese and animal ingredients

Some hard cheeses, including traditional parmesan, are made with animal rennet. If that matters to you, read our vegetarian and vegan options guide and ask us before you order, because we cannot confirm the rennet source for every cheese.
