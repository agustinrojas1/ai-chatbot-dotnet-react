Our parking lot is next to the restaurant, and it is the easiest way to collect a pickup order or reach the side door. This page covers parking, access and the best place to meet us.

## Parking lot

Guests can park in our lot, which has about 30 spaces. Two of them are reserved for accessible parking and are closest to the side door. Parking is free during opening hours, and the lot is not monitored overnight.

## Pickup at the side door

Pickup orders are handed over at the side door, which faces the parking lot. Show the order number from your WhatsApp chat, and the team hands over the bag. Please do not block the door, because drivers use the same space for deliveries.

## Arriving by bike, taxi or on foot

The bike rack is next to the side door and holds up to 8 bikes. Taxi and rideshare drivers can drop guests at the front entrance, but they should not wait in the loading zone for more than 5 minutes.
