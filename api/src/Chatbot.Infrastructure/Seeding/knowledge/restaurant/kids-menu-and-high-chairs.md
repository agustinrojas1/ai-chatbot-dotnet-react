## No separate kids menu

We do not have a separate kids menu. Children are welcome to share a pizza from the regular menu, and we are happy to cut it into smaller slices. Ask for an extra plate when you order, and we will bring one with cutlery at no charge.

Because our menu has no kids-only items, children's meals are priced the same as adult portions. We do not offer kids discounts.

## High chairs

We have two high chairs, available on a first-come basis in the dining room and on the patio. We do not have booster seats. If you need a high chair, ask the host when you arrive.

High chairs are not included with delivery or pickup, since they stay in the restaurant.

## Allergies for children

Children with food allergies should follow the same guidance as adults. Read our allergens page before ordering, and write the allergy in the order notes so the kitchen can take it into account.
