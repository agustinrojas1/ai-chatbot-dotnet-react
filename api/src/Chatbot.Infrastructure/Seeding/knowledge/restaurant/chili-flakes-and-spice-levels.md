Some of our dishes are spicy, and you can choose the heat level that suits you. Here is how we handle spice in our kitchen.

## Which dishes are spicy

Our Gluten-Free Penne Arrabbiata has the most heat on the menu. It is made with chili and garlic in a tomato sauce. Our pizzas are mild by default, and you can add chili flakes to any of them on request.

## Chili flakes on the side

Chili flakes sit in a small shaker on each dining table. For pickup and delivery, we add a chili flakes pack to the bag if you ask for it when you order. We do not add extra chili to any dish unless you request it, and the shaker holds about 30 grams.

## Asking for less or more heat

Tell us your preferred level when you order: mild, medium or hot. For the Arrabbiata, we can make it milder by using less chili, and we can add extra chili on request. We cannot guarantee an exact heat level, because the strength of chili varies from batch to batch.
