We read every comment we receive, from WhatsApp messages to online reviews. Your feedback helps us keep our menu and service consistent.

## Sending feedback

Send feedback on WhatsApp after your order, or email help@luigispizzeria.example. If your comment is about a specific order, include the order number so the team can find it quickly. Compliments are passed on to the kitchen and to the staff on shift. You can also ask a host for a paper comment card at the counter, and the cards are read every Friday.

## Our response time

A team member replies within 2 business days to comments that need an answer. Refund requests follow the rules in our late or wrong order refunds guide, and they are handled within 7 business days.

## Online reviews

We do not offer discounts or free food in exchange for reviews, and we do not remove negative reviews. If a review breaks a platform's rules, we report it through that platform rather than changing it ourselves.
