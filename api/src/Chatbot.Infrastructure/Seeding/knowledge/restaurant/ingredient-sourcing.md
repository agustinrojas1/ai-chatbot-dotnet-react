## Flour

Our pizza dough is made with Molino Dorato Type 00 flour, a finely milled Italian-style flour that gives a soft, airy crust. Our gluten-free dough uses a separate gluten-free flour blend, which is not the same flour used for the regular crust.

## Cheese

Fresh mozzarella comes from Caseificio Valle Verde and is delivered every Tuesday and Friday. We keep it in the walk-in cooler and use it within a few days of delivery. Parmesan, gorgonzola and fontina are bought from specialty importers and stored separately.

## Tomatoes, herbs and produce

Our tomato sauce is cooked in-house from whole peeled tomatoes, with garlic, salt and basil. Basil and seasonal vegetables such as bell peppers and red onion are bought fresh several times a week. When a product changes, we update the ingredient list on the menu.
