We keep our takeout and delivery packaging simple and recyclable. Here is what we use and how you can recycle it.

## Pizza boxes and bags

Pizzas come in plain cardboard boxes that can be recycled once you remove any food residue. Delivery orders travel in insulated bags, which drivers bring back so we can reuse them. We do not add a packaging fee to any order.

## Cutlery, napkins and sauce

Disposable plates and napkins are made from unbleached paper, and each delivery bag holds up to 4 pizza boxes. Cutlery is available on request only, so you do not receive utensils you do not need. Sauce cups are added only when you ask for them.

## Recycling tips

Clean and dry cardboard boxes can go in the paper recycling bin. Boxes with grease stains should go in the compost or general waste bin. Please empty and rinse any Limonata bottle before recycling it.
