## Cancel at no charge

You can cancel an order at no charge within 3 minutes after confirming it. After that, the order has already entered the kitchen and can no longer be cancelled. If there is a problem with what you received, see our refund policy.

## Add or remove items

You can add items up to 10 minutes after confirming the order. Removing an item, or switching the dough to a gluten-free (sin TACC) version, can be done within the same time limit, as long as the item is not yet in the oven.

If the change has an additional cost, we tell you before confirming the new total, and it does not apply until you accept it.

## How to request a change

Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and what you want to change. A team member reviews the request. Address changes are only possible if the new address is within our delivery zone.
