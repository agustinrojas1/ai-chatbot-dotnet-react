Our restrooms are for guests dining in or waiting for a pickup order. Here is what you can find and when you can use them.

## Restrooms

We have two single-occupancy restrooms, one for men and one for women, both located off the hallway between the dining room and the kitchen. Restrooms are open during our regular hours, from 12:00 to 22:00. Each one has a sink, a mirror, liquid soap and a hand dryer, and the team restocks paper towels at the start of every shift.

## Baby changing

A baby changing table is in the accessible restroom, which is larger than the others. There is no separate family restroom. Disposable changing pads are available on request, and a host brings them to your table.

## Cleaning and problems

Restrooms are checked and cleaned every hour during service. If you notice a problem, tell a host at the counter, and we will fix it as soon as possible.
