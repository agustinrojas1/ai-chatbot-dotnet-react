## How points are earned

You earn 1 point for every dollar spent on paid orders. We do not count tips or taxes. Points are credited when the order is delivered or picked up, and you do not need to download any app: you sign up with your WhatsApp number.

## Redeeming points

With 250 points you can redeem a dessert from the menu at no charge. You can make one redemption per order, and the dessert can be added as an item in the same cart.

## Expiry

Points expire 12 months after your last purchase. Before they expire, we remind you on WhatsApp so you can redeem them. If you cancel a paid order, the points associated with that order are deducted.

If you have questions about your balance, message us on WhatsApp with the number you signed up with. We reply with your available total and the expiry date of each batch of points.
