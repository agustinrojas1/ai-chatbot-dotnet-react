Food safety is our first priority in the kitchen. Our team follows daily routines for cooking, storing and cleaning, and we check them every shift.

## Cold storage and temperatures

The walk-in cooler is kept at 3 °C (38 °F) or below, and its temperature is logged twice per shift. Fresh mozzarella is stored in the cooler and used within a few days of delivery. Hard cheeses are stored separately from fresh produce.

## Cleaning and handwashing

Staff wash their hands before starting a shift, after handling raw ingredients and after using the restroom. Work surfaces are sanitized every 2 hours during service. Cutting boards are color-coded, with red for raw meat and green for vegetables, so the two never mix.

## Inspections and complaints

The restaurant is inspected by the local health department at least once a year, and the latest report is available at the host stand. If you think a dish made you unwell, tell us on WhatsApp the same day with your order number. We keep a record of each batch for 30 days and review it with you.
