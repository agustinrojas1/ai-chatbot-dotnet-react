## Allergens by dish

Every pizza on our menu contains gluten in the regular crust and dairy in the cheese. For a gluten-free crust, see our Spanish guide on the gluten-free crust and cross-contamination.

- Prosciutto & Mushroom: gluten, dairy and pork.
- Garlic Bread: gluten and dairy (butter).
- Tiramisu: gluten, dairy and eggs.
- Caprese Salad: dairy.
- Gluten-Free Penne Arrabbiata and Limonata: no gluten, dairy or eggs listed in their recipes.

If you have an allergy we do not list here, ask us before you order.

## Nut policy

We use no tree nuts in any recipe, and we do not buy nut flours, pesto or praline. Our Tiramisu uses cocoa powder, not nuts.

We do not run a nut-free kitchen, though. Nuts are not stored at our counter, but we cannot guarantee that a dish is free of traces, and we cannot certify our kitchen as nut-free.

## Reporting an allergy

Write the allergy in the order notes so the kitchen sees it on the ticket. For a severe allergy, we recommend choosing a dish with a short ingredient list and reading the tags on each item when it arrives. When in doubt, do not eat it.
