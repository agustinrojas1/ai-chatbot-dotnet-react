## Table reservations

We do not take table reservations by WhatsApp or by phone. We seat guests on a first-come, first-served basis, both inside the restaurant and on the patio.

## Groups of 8 or more

If your group is 8 people or more, please let us know 6 hours in advance so we can coordinate the kitchen and the timing. Groups can place one group order on a single ticket, with the bill split however they prefer when paying.

For group orders that are taken away to another place, pickup at the restaurant works the same as an individual order, but please tell us the exact time.

## Fixed-time events

If your group needs food served at a set time at a place other than the restaurant, the best option is catering. For that case, see our catering and party platters guide.
