Some addresses are hard to find, especially apartment complexes with gates or buildings with unclear numbering. Here is how to help our drivers reach you quickly.

## Gate codes and entry

Include your gate or building code in the address notes when you order. Codes are kept private and are deleted from our system 24 hours after delivery.

## When the driver cannot find you

If the driver cannot find the address, they call or message you on WhatsApp. They wait up to 10 minutes at the address before returning to the restaurant. If the order is returned, a new delivery is charged the standard fee, and the address must still be within 5 km.

## Making the address clear

Give the building name, the floor and a nearby landmark. Write the address exactly as it appears on the street sign. Check the location pin in WhatsApp before you confirm, because distance is measured from the restaurant to your address by road, not in a straight line.
