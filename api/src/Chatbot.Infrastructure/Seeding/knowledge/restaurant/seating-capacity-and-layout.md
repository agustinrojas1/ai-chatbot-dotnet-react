Our restaurant has a dining room and a patio, plus a back room for private events. This page explains how many guests we can seat and how the space is set up.

## Dining room and patio

The dining room seats 60 guests at tables for two to six. The patio seats 24 guests when the weather is good, and it is open from April to October, as explained in our dog-friendly patio guide.

## Back room

The back room seats up to 28 guests for private events. It is available for dinner service only, as described in our private events and birthdays guide.

## Walk-ins and peak hours

Tables are given on a first-come, first-served basis, and the dining room is busiest between 19:00 and 21:00. During peak hours, expect to wait up to 20 minutes for a table. We do not hold tables for guests who are late, and a party of 6 or more may need to wait for two tables to be cleared.
