Before you buy, you can commission an independent technical inspection in addition to the notary's checks. The notary reviews the legal title, while an inspector checks the physical condition of the property.

## What the inspector checks

A standard inspection covers the structure, damp, the roof, the electrical installation, gas fittings and plumbing. The inspector takes photos and writes a report, which usually arrives within 5 business days of the visit.

## Cost and time on site

A standard inspection of a 2- or 3-room apartment costs between USD 250 and USD 400, paid by the buyer. The visit usually takes about 2 hours on site. Larger houses take longer and cost more, so ask for a quote before you book.

## When to book it

Book the inspection after your offer is accepted and before you sign the purchase agreement (boleto de compraventa). That way the findings can be used in the negotiation, before the 10% deposit (seña) is paid.
