## Your lease survives a sale

A sale does not automatically end your lease. The new owner takes over the contract on the same terms until its end date, including the agreed rent and the deposit. You do not need to move out because the building changed hands.

## Viewings by buyers

Buyers may visit the unit while you live there, but the owner must give you 48 hours' notice before each viewing. We limit viewings to two per week, between 10:00 and 18:00, and you can ask us to reschedule a viewing that clashes with work or travel.

## If you are asked to leave

The new owner can only end the lease under the terms written in the contract. A request to leave is not a termination. If you receive one, send it to us in writing so we can review the clause with you.
