## Lease length

Rental contracts run for 24 months, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing. At expiry, if neither party gives notice that it will not renew, the contract is extended for the same period and on the same terms, with any applicable adjustments.

## Early termination penalty

If the tenant leaves before completing 6 months, they pay a penalty of 2 months' rent. If they leave after 6 months and before the expiry date, the penalty drops to 1 month's rent.

## How to give notice

Termination is communicated in writing to the agency with 45 days' notice. The period is counted from the day we receive the notice. We ask you to coordinate the key handover and the unit inspection well in advance.
