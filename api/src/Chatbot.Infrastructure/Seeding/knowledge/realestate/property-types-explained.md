Listings use local names for common types of home. This page explains what each one means, so you know what to expect before a viewing.

## Apartments, studios and lofts

An apartment (departamento) is a unit inside a multi-storey building, and its size is given by the number of rooms. A studio is a single room with a kitchen corner and a bathroom, counted as 1 room. A loft is a converted industrial space with high ceilings and open layouts, often with exposed brick walls.

## Townhouses (PH)

A PH (propiedad horizontal) is a unit with its own entrance in a low-rise building, sharing walls and a courtyard with a few neighbors. Each listing states whether building fees (expensas) apply, so check it before you compare prices.

## Houses

A house is a stand-alone building with its own land and entrance, and it usually has more rooms than an apartment. Our houses have between 4 and 5 rooms, and some include a private garden or a grill area.
