Internet and cable TV are not included in the rent. You arrange them yourself with a provider that serves your building, in the same way as electricity, gas and water.

## Choosing a provider

Ask us before move-in which internet and cable providers serve the building, because coverage can vary by street and by floor. We confirm the options for your building before the handover, so you can compare plans while you still have time.

## Installation time

Internet providers usually need 3 to 10 business days to install a new line. Order the service as soon as you have a signed contract, so that you are connected by your first week in the unit.

## Cabling and wall works

Drilling for cable outlets or running cables along the walls needs the owner's written approval, as described in our renovations policy. Cables may not be fixed to the building's façade or the common areas without the building's approval.
