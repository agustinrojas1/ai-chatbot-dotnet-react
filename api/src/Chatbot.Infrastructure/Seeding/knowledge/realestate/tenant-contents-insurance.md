Our contracts do not require contents insurance, but we recommend it. It protects your belongings if they are damaged, stolen or lost. It is separate from the rental bond insurance (seguro de caución), which is the guarantee some tenants use instead of an owner guarantee.

## What it covers

A contents policy typically covers your furniture, electronics and clothing against fire, water damage from your unit, and theft. Many policies also include liability for damage you cause to a neighbor's unit, such as a leak that spreads to the flat below.

## What to check before you buy

Ask for a policy that covers the replacement value of your belongings, not their second-hand value. Check the deductible (the amount you pay per claim) and whether the policy covers items you keep in the storage unit, if your listing includes one.

## If you choose bond insurance

Rental bond insurance taken out as a guarantee runs for the full 24-month term of the contract. It is a different product from contents insurance, so buying one does not replace the other.
