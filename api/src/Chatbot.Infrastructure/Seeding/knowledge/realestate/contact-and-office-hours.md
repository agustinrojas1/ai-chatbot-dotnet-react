This chat is the main way to reach PrimeHaven Realty. You can ask about listings, request a viewing, send documents, or follow up on an application without leaving the conversation.

## Office hours

Our team answers during business hours, Monday to Saturday, from 10:00 to 18:00, Buenos Aires time. Messages sent outside those hours are answered the next business day. Viewings follow the same window, so the team is available when you come to see a property.

## Which channel to use

Use this chat for viewings, cancellations, applications and general questions, because it keeps a record of your request. Notices that a contract requires, such as a termination notice, must be sent in writing to the agency, as explained in our lease policy.

## Response times

Routine questions get a reply within the same business day. Document reviews take up to 3 business days. For an emergency in the unit, such as a water leak or a smell of gas, follow the instructions in our maintenance policy first, then contact us through this chat.
