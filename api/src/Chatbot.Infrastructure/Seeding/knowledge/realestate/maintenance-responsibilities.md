## Who pays for what

Tenants pay for small repairs up to USD 80 per incident, such as replacing light bulbs, clearing a drain with a plunger or changing a shower head. Owners cover structural repairs, the roof, main plumbing and wiring, and major appliances that fail from age.

## How to report a problem

Report any problem in writing within 72 hours of noticing it, with photos if possible. Early reports keep small problems small. If a delay makes the damage worse, the extra cost may fall on you.

## Emergencies

For a water leak or a smell of gas, act first: shut off the main valve, and leave the unit if you smell gas. Then call the emergency number printed on your contract. Tell us afterwards so we can log the incident and inform the owner.
