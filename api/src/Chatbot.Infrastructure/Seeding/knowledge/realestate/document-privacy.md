Applications require personal documents, such as your ID and payslips. This page explains who can see them, how long we keep them, and what you can ask us to do with them.

## Who can see your documents

Only the agency staff who handle your application can see your documents, along with the insurer you choose for a bond policy. The owner receives a summary of your application and does not receive copies of your ID or payslips unless you agree in writing.

## How long we keep them

If an application is rejected or withdrawn, we delete the documents within 90 days. For signed contracts, we keep copies for the length of the contract plus 5 years, so that disputes about the agreement can be resolved.

## Your rights

Under Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law (Ley 25.326), you can ask in writing what personal data we hold about you and ask us to correct it. We answer within 10 business days. You can also ask us to delete data that we no longer need for an application or a contract.
