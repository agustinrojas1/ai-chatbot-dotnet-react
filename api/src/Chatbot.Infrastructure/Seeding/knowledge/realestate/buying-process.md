## Steps of the purchase

1. You choose the property and submit a written offer. If the seller accepts it, the final terms are negotiated.
2. You sign the purchase agreement (boleto de compraventa) with a 10% deposit (seña) on the price. The agreement sets the date of the deed and the financing conditions, if there are any.
3. The deed is signed before a notary (escribano). Beforehand, the notary reviews the seller's title, debts and restrictions (inhibiciones).

## Taxes and fees

The notary's fees usually range from 1% to 2% of the price, and the buyer pays them. The property transfer tax (impuesto a la transferencia de inmuebles, ITI) is 1.5% of the price and is generally paid by the seller. Stamp duties (sellos) are around 3.6% of the tax value and are usually the buyer's responsibility; the notary confirms the exact amount for the jurisdiction.

## Timing and recommendations

Allow between 30 and 60 days from the purchase agreement to the deed, depending on the financing. Ask for a copy of the title and the debt certificates from the start, so you reach signing without surprises. Our agency fees are explained in their own document.
