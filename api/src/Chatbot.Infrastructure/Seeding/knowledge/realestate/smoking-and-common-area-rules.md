Halls, stairwells, the lift and the bin room are shared by all residents. The rules on this page apply to tenants and to their guests.

## Smoking

Smoking is not allowed in common areas, in stairwells, in the lift, or on the rooftop terrace. Smoking inside the unit is allowed only if the owner agrees in writing, because smoke can stain paint and damage fittings. Smoke damage found at move-out is charged at the cost of the repair.

## Keeping common areas clear

Do not leave bicycles, strollers, furniture or boxes in halls or stairwells, since they block emergency exits. Items left in common areas are removed by the caretaker after 48 hours, and the cost of removal may be charged to the unit's tenant.

## Reporting problems

If you see a shared area that is damaged or unsafe, such as a broken light in a stairwell, report it to us within 72 hours. We pass the report to the building administrator and log it, as we do for other maintenance issues.
