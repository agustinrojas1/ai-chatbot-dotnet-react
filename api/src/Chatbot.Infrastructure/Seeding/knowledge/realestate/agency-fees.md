## Rentals

The tenant pays 4% of the total contract value plus VAT (IVA). The total contract value is the monthly rent multiplied by the number of months in the contract. The owner also pays 4% of the total contract value plus VAT, so the commission is shared between both parties.

## Sales

In sales, the buyer and the seller each pay 3% of the sale price plus VAT. Fees are paid when the purchase agreement (boleto de compraventa) is signed, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

## What we do not charge

We do not charge for the initial consultation, for viewings, or for preparing the paperwork. If the deal does not go through, no fee is payable.

Each party receives its own invoice for the fee that corresponds to it. If you have questions about the calculation, ask us to show you the full breakdown before you sign, so there are no surprises on the day of the transaction.
