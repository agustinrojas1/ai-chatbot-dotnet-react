A viewing lasts 30 minutes, so it helps to know in advance what to look at. This checklist covers the points tenants and buyers ask about most often.

## Water, power and signal

Turn on every tap and let the hot water run for one minute to check the pressure and how quickly it heats up. Open the electrical panel and check that the breakers are labelled. Test the mobile signal in each room, since it can be weak in some interiors.

## Windows, noise and light

Check whether the windows are double-glazed and whether they close properly. Listen for street noise at the time of your visit, and ideally visit at the hour you would normally be home. Notice how much daylight the main room gets in the late morning.

## Building and documents

Ask for the most recent building fees statement (expensas) and read the building's notice board for quiet hours and shared-space rules. If you are considering a purchase, ask for the title and debt certificates as well, so you can start the notary's checks early.
