## Which buildings accept pets

Each listing states whether the building accepts pets. Some co-ownership rules (reglamentos de copropiedad) allow them and others do not, and in those cases there is no exception, even if the tenant is willing to pay more. If the listing does not say, ask us before you book a viewing.

We do not ban breeds. What we do measure is size: we accept pets up to 10 kg of adult weight. Dogs, cats, rabbits and small birds fall into that category without a problem.

## Deposit and responsibilities

When the building accepts pets, an additional deposit of USD 150 per pet is added. It is returned at the end of the contract if the unit has no damage attributable to the pet. It does not replace the general rental deposit.

The tenant is responsible for damage, noise and any fine the building's association (consorcio) imposes because of the pet.

## Documentation

Before signing, we ask for an up-to-date rabies vaccination card and, for dogs, the municipal registration certificate. If your pet is over the weight limit, let us know anyway: we can show you another property in our portfolio that better matches what you are looking for.
