Most buildings have a bin room on the ground floor. Following the same few rules keeps it clean for everyone and avoids warnings from the building administrator.

## Collection times

Household garbage goes out in sealed bags between 19:00 and 22:00 on collection days. Never leave bags in the hallway or next to the bins; they must go inside the bin room. The building's cleaning service washes the bin room every Monday and Thursday, so keep the floor around the bins clear on those mornings.

## Recycling

Separate paper and cardboard, plastic and cans, and glass. Glass goes in its own container. Rinse recyclables and flatten boxes before putting them in the bin, and never use plastic bags for recyclable material.

## Bulky items

Furniture, appliances and building materials are not collected with household waste. The tenant pays for the removal of bulky items such as old furniture or appliances, and the building administrator can arrange a collection on request.
