## Handover day

Keys are handed over from 14:00 on your start date, either at our office or at the unit with an agent present. Bring your ID and the receipt for your first payment. We do not hand over keys without both.

## How many keys you get

You receive three sets of keys: two for the unit and one for the building's access card or fob. Extra copies must be cut by the locksmith we approve, not at a random shop, to keep the lock secure.

## Lost keys and lock changes

A lost set is charged at USD 40 per set. If the keys are not recovered, the lock is changed and the cost is charged to the tenant. Lock changes requested by the tenant need the owner's written approval first.
