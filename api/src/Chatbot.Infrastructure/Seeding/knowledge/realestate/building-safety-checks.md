Buildings with gas installations, fire equipment and water tanks have scheduled safety checks. We let you know in advance when a check needs access to your unit.

## Scheduled checks

Gas installations are inspected once a year by a licensed gas fitter. Fire extinguishers in common areas are checked every 6 months, and so are the water tanks, which are cleaned at the same time.

## Access to your unit

When a check needs access to your unit, we ask for written notice at least 24 hours in advance. Visits are arranged between 09:00 and 17:00 on a weekday, and you may ask us to move the time if it clashes with work or travel.

## Smoke detectors

We ask tenants to test the smoke detector once a month by pressing its test button. If it beeps or does not respond, report the fault within 72 hours, so that it can be replaced before it is needed.
