Some buildings offer shared spaces, such as a rooftop terrace or a grill area. Each listing says which spaces come with the unit, and access to them follows the building's rules.

## Booking a shared space

Shared spaces are booked through the building administrator at least 48 hours in advance. Each booking covers one slot of 3 hours and a maximum of 12 people. Bookings are confirmed in writing before the day of use.

## Opening hours

The rooftop terrace is open daily from 09:00 to 22:00. The grill area can be used until 21:00, so that it is clear before the quiet hours begin. Whoever books the space must leave it clean, with the grill cooled and the bins closed.

## Cancellations

A booking can be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the slot. Cancellations made later than that may be charged a cleaning fee of USD 30, and the slot is then released for other residents.
