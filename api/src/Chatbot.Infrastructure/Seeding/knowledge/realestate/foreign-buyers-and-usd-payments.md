## Buying as a foreigner

Foreign nationals can buy property in Argentina without holding local residency. You will need a valid passport and a tax identification number, either a CUIT or a CDI. The notary will ask for both before the deed is signed, so start that process early.

## Paying in US dollars

We accept bank wire transfers in USD and, for in-person payments, cash in US dollars. Cash payments in USD are accepted up to USD 5,000 per operation. Larger amounts must go through a bank transfer or be paid through the notary's escrow at closing.

## Source-of-funds checks

Real estate transactions fall under Argentina's anti-money-laundering rules (UIF). Expect questions about where your funds come from. The notary may request bank statements or tax filings from your home country. Gather them early, since this step is the most common cause of closing delays.
