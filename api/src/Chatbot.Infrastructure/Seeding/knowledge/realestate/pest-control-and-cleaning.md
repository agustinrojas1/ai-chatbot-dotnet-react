Buildings with shared areas schedule pest control and cleaning through the building administrator. This page explains what the building covers and what you need to report.

## Building pest control

Common areas, such as the bin room and the basement, are treated for pests every 3 months by the administrator's contractor. You are notified at least 48 hours before each treatment, so you can keep the area clear on the day.

## Your part

Store food in sealed containers and take out garbage in closed bags, following our garbage and recycling rules. Report signs of pests, such as droppings or sightings, within 72 hours, the same deadline as other maintenance problems.

## Inside the unit

Treating pests inside the unit is the tenant's responsibility, unless the infestation came from the building or from a structural defect. In those cases the owner pays, as explained in our maintenance policy, and we need the report in writing with photos.
