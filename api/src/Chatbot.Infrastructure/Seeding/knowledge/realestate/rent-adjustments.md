## How often the rent changes

Under our standard contracts, rent adjusts every 4 months. Each adjustment uses the ICL (Índice de Contratos de Locación), the index published by the Central Bank of Argentina (BCRA), for the period since the last change. The contract may name a different index if both parties agree in writing.

## How we notify you

We send written notice at least 15 days before the new amount takes effect. The notice includes the index values used and the calculation, so you can check the math yourself. The new amount appears on your next rent receipt.

## Rules that apply

Adjustments follow the rules in force on the signing date and the terms of your contract. This page is general information, not legal advice. Before signing, ask us to walk you through the adjustment clause, and feel free to have your own lawyer review it.
