Some listings include extras such as a storage unit or a shared laundry room. Check the listing: an extra is only included when it is named there, and its use follows the building's rules.

## Storage units

A storage unit is a closed cage or locker in the building's basement, usually between 1 and 2 square metres. Store only household items in it. Gas cylinders and fuel cans are not allowed in storage units or in the laundry room, for safety reasons.

## Laundry rooms

Laundry rooms are open from 07:00 to 21:00. A washer cycle takes about 60 minutes. Dryers are used on a first-come, first-served basis, and the room must be left clean for the next person.

## Keys and access

Storage units and laundry rooms are opened with the same access card or fob as the building entrance. If that card is lost, the replacement is charged as a lost set of keys, at USD 40.
