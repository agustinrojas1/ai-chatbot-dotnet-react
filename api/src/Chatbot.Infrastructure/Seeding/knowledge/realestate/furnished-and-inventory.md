## Furnished vs unfurnished

Furnished units come with the items listed on the inventory: beds, a sofa, a table and chairs, kitchen appliances and basic kitchenware. Unfurnished units include built-in kitchen and bathroom fixtures and, where the listing states it, air conditioning. Anything not on the list is not included.

## Signing the inventory

Before you move in, an agent walks through the unit with you and records the condition of every room, with photos. Sign the inventory within 2 business days of move-in. If we do not receive a signed copy in that time, the list is treated as accepted.

## Returning the unit

At move-out, the unit must be returned in the same condition as the inventory, allowing for normal wear. Replacing or removing a furnished item requires written approval from the owner. Missing items are charged at the replacement value shown in the inventory.
