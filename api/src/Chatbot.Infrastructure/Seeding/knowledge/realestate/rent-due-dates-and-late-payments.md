Rent is due on the first day of each month. This page explains the grace period and what happens when a payment is late.

## Due date and grace period

Rent is due on the 1st of each month. A payment received up to the 5th counts as on time. Building fees (expensas) are a separate monthly charge, due on the 10th.

## Late payments

From the 6th, a late charge of 2% of the monthly rent applies for each week of delay, capped at 10% of the monthly rent. The late charge is added to your next rent receipt, so the amount is always visible.

## Telling us in advance

If you expect to pay late, tell us in writing before the due date. We send a written reminder to any tenant whose payment has not been received by the 6th, and the reminder states the amount due and the late charge that applies.
