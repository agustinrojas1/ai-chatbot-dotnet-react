## What preapproval is

Preapproval is an assessment the bank makes of your ability to pay. It is valid for 90 days; after that it must be updated. It is worth having before you submit an offer, because the seller takes it as a sign of seriousness.

## How much you can finance

Banks usually finance up to 70% of the appraised value, with terms of up to 20 years. Rates change often, so ask for a simulation that shows the total financial cost (CFT, costo financiero total), and compare at least two banks before you decide.
