## Doorman and amenities

Where a building has a doorman, the desk is staffed from 07:00 to 23:00 on weekdays and from 08:00 to 20:00 on weekends. Buildings with a gym open it daily from 06:30 to 22:30 for residents. Hours can change for maintenance, and the building notice board is the reference.

## Accessibility

Each listing states whether it has step-free street access, a lift and doors at least 80 cm wide. If you need a specific adaptation, tell us at the first contact so we can check the building's rules before you visit.

## Working from home and professional use

Home-office work is fine as long as it creates no client traffic. Seeing clients, running a clinic or storing stock in the unit needs the owner's written consent, and the owner may refuse if the building's rules do not allow it.
