We rent and sell homes in several Buenos Aires neighborhoods. Listings are described by type and location, and we share the exact address only after the first contact.

## North of the city

Palermo, Belgrano, Recoleta and Núñez are in the north of the city. Belgrano has both apartments and houses, Palermo has apartments for rent, and Recoleta has furnished studios for rent. Núñez has houses with 5 rooms.

## West and center

Almagro, Caballito and Villa Crespo are in the west and center. Almagro has a townhouse (PH) for rent, Caballito has apartments with laundry rooms, and Villa Crespo has apartments with a shared rooftop terrace.

## South of the city

San Telmo is in the south of the city. Our loft listings are there, and lofts in San Telmo usually have high ceilings and exposed brick walls. Tell us your budget and preferred neighborhoods, and we will suggest up to 3 listings that fit.
