Companies can rent a unit for employees, executives or project staff. The contract is signed by the company, and every occupant must be named in it.

## Documents for the company

We need the company's tax ID (CUIT), its articles of incorporation (estatuto), and the document that shows who has authority to sign for it. The legal representative must also present a valid national ID or passport. We review the documents within 3 business days of receiving them.

## Guarantee for companies

Companies usually provide rental bond insurance (seguro de caución) for the full 24-month term of the contract, instead of an owner guarantee. The 3-times-rent income test applies to the company's verified monthly revenue.

## Occupants and sublets

Only the people named in the contract may live in the unit. Changing an occupant requires a written request to us, and the company remains responsible for the unit and for the rent. Subletting is not allowed under any of our contracts, so a company cannot pass the unit on to another firm.
