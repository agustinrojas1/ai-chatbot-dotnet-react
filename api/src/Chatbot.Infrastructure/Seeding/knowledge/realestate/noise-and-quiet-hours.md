Every building we rent in has rules on noise, because the units share walls, floors and stairwells with neighbors. The rules apply to all tenants and to their guests.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours run from 22:00 to 08:00 on weekdays and from 23:00 to 09:00 on weekends. During those hours, avoid loud music, television at high volume and any work that makes noise inside the unit.

## Gatherings and guests

Gatherings of more than 10 guests should be announced to the building's front desk at least 24 hours in advance, where the building has one. Guests who stay longer than 14 consecutive days need the owner's written approval, as explained in our subletting policy.

## Complaints and fines

When a neighbor complains about noise, the building administrator contacts the tenant in writing, usually after a first warning. Repeated complaints can lead to a fine imposed by the building's association (consorcio). Any fine is the tenant's responsibility.
