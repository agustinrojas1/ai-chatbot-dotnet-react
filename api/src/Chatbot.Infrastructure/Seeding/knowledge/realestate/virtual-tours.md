If you cannot visit in person, we can offer a live video tour of the unit. A video tour follows the same booking rules as an in-person viewing, so it is easy to compare the two.

## How a video tour works

A video tour lasts 30 minutes, the same as an in-person viewing, and is booked in the same window: Monday to Saturday, from 10:00 to 18:00, Buenos Aires time. We walk through the unit with a phone or tablet and show each room, the balcony or patio, and the building entrance.

## What to have ready

Have your national ID (DNI) or passport at hand, since we check it at the start of the call, as we do for in-person viewings. Each video tour is for one group only, and the call is limited to 4 people in total, children included.

## Limits

A video tour does not replace a visit before you sign a contract. Noise, smell and natural light are hard to judge on screen, so we recommend at least one visit on site before you sign.
