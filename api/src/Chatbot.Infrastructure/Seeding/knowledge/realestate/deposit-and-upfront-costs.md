## Security deposit

When you sign, the security deposit (depósito en garantía) equals one month of rent. It is returned within 30 calendar days after the keys are handed over, less any pending services and any damage that applies, supported by receipts.

## What you pay at signing

When you sign, you pay the first month of rent in advance, together with the deposit. If agency fees apply, they are paid at that same moment (see the agency fees policy). The building fees (expensas) for the first month are charged separately, prorated for the days you occupy the unit.

If you choose rental bond insurance (seguro de caución), the premium is paid when the policy is issued and is not included in the deposit.

## How you pay and receive receipts

We accept bank transfers to the administration's account. Each payment generates a receipt that arrives by email the same day. Keep the transfer confirmation: it is the only thing we need to credit your deposit when it is returned.
