## What they cover

Building fees (expensas) are the building's shared costs: the doorman, cleaning of common areas, the lift, building insurance, the caretaker's salary, and maintenance of pumps and water tanks. Each unit pays its share based on the floor area it occupies, as stated in the building's co-ownership rules (reglamento de copropiedad).

## Who pays what

Ordinary building fees are paid by the tenant every month, together with the rent. Extraordinary ones, for example replacing a lift or repairing the façade, are paid by the owner, unless the contract says otherwise. We put this in writing so there is no doubt.

## When they are due

Building fees are due on the 10th of each month, and the receipt arrives by email. The amount varies by building and with each statement issued by the building administrator. If you want, we can request the latest statement before you sign, so you know exactly what you are signing up for.
