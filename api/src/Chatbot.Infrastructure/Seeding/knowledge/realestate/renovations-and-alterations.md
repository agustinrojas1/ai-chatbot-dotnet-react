Any change to a rental unit needs the owner's written approval, including small changes such as painting a wall in a new colour. Make the request before you start, not after.

## What needs approval

Painting in a new colour, drilling holes for shelves or a TV, changing light fixtures, and replacing built-in fittings such as the kitchen sink or bathroom tiles all need the owner's written approval before work begins. Routine small repairs, covered in our maintenance policy, do not.

## Work hours and notice

Approved works may run only on weekdays from 09:00 to 17:00 and on Saturdays from 09:00 to 13:00. Send the request at least 10 business days before the work is due to start, so we can coordinate it with the owner and the building.

## Costs and move-out

Works paid for by the tenant are not reimbursed and are not deducted from the deposit unless the owner agrees in writing before the work starts. Changes you make must be reversed at move-out, unless the owner agrees in writing to keep them.
