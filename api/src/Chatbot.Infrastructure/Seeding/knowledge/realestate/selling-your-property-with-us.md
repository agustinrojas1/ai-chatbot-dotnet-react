If you want to sell your home, we handle the listing, the photos and the visits from interested buyers. Our fee for sellers is 3% of the sale price plus VAT, as explained in our agency fees policy.

## Preparing the listing

We take professional photos of the property and write the listing with the number of rooms, the neighborhood, the building type and the main features. Before we publish, we ask for the title, the latest building fees statement (expensas) and the debt certificates, so buyers see accurate information from the start.

## Publishing and visits

Your listing goes live within 5 business days of signing the listing agreement. Buyers visit by appointment, Monday to Saturday between 10:00 and 18:00, following the same schedule as our viewings. If you prefer fewer visits, you can ask us to cap them at two per week.

## Fees and paperwork

The seller's fee is 3% of the sale price plus VAT, and the buyer pays the same percentage. Fees are paid when the purchase agreement (boleto de compraventa) is signed. Once a buyer makes an offer, the steps follow our step-by-step buying process.
