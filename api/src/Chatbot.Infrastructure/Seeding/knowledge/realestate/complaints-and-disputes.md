If something goes wrong during a rental or a purchase, tell us in writing. This page explains how complaints are logged and answered, and where to go if we cannot resolve the issue together.

## Sending a complaint

Write to us in this chat or by email, describing what happened, the date, and any photos or receipts. We confirm in writing that we have received the complaint within 2 business days.

## Response times

We reply with a proposed solution within 10 business days. If the issue is complex, we tell you the reason for any delay. If we cannot resolve it directly, we offer a meeting with the owner, or with an independent mediator if both sides agree.

## Outside help

You may take a complaint to the consumer protection office of the city of Buenos Aires. Our contracts are governed by Argentine law, and this page is general information, not legal advice. If a dispute is serious, you can consult your own lawyer before signing or responding.
