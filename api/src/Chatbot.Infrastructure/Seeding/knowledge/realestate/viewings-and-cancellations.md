## How a viewing works

Viewings last 30 minutes and are scheduled Monday to Saturday, from 10:00 to 18:00, Buenos Aires time. Each slot is for one group only, so book the time that suits you.

## What to bring

When you arrive you must present your national ID (DNI) or passport; without a document we cannot open the unit. Each viewing is limited to 4 people, children included. If there are more, they can split into separate slots, or let us know in advance so we can see whether we can arrange a second viewing.

## Cancellations

If you cannot attend, cancel at least 24 hours in advance by replying in this same chat. That frees the slot for someone else. A viewing without confirmation from our team does not hold a reserved place until we validate it.
