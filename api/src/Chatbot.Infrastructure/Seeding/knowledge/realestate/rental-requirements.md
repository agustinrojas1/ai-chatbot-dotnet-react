## Documentation

To rent, we need a valid national ID (DNI) or passport, your last 6 payslips or, if you are a monotributista (self-employed), your last 6 invoices issued, and your tax ID certificate (CUIT or CUIL). We review documents within 3 business days of receiving them.

## Income and guarantee

Verifiable monthly income must be at least 3 times the rent. If you do not reach that amount, you can provide an owner guarantee (garantía propietaria): a property owner in the area vouches for the contract with their own property, which must be free of debts and seizures (embargos).

The other option is rental bond insurance (seguro de caución), which is taken out for the full length of the contract. We work with Seguros Andina Caución and Aseguradora del Plata. The insurer assesses your profile, and the quote arrives by email before you sign.

## Before signing

With the documentation complete, we coordinate the contract signing and the initial payment. If you are unsure which guarantee suits you best, tell us your situation and we will advise you with no obligation.
