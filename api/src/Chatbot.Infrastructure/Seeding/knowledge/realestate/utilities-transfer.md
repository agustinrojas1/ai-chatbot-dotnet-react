## Services to transfer

Electricity, gas and water are not included in the rent and must be transferred to your name. Electricity is supplied by Edenor or Edesur depending on the zone, gas by Metrogas or Naturgy BAN, and water and sewage by AySA. We confirm which providers serve your building before move-in.

## Deadlines

Transfer the AySA water account within 15 days of move-in. Until then the account stays in the owner's name, and any charges for that period are yours to reimburse.

## Meter readings

Take a photo of each meter on move-in day and send it to us with the inventory. Readings taken at handover prevent disputes about who used what. Keep copies of every transfer confirmation as well, since those receipts are the proof we need if a bill arrives in the wrong name.
