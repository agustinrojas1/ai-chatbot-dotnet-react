## Subletting

Subletting is not allowed under any of our contracts. That includes renting a room to a friend or relative who is not named on the lease. If you need an exception, ask in writing. Approval is at the owner's discretion and is never assumed.

## Short-term rentals

Airbnb, Booking.com and similar platforms are prohibited in all our units, for any length of stay. Breaking this rule is a breach of contract: the owner can end the lease and charge a penalty of three months' rent.

## Guests

Visits from friends and family are fine, as long as no guest stays more than 14 consecutive days. Longer stays count as occupancy by a new person and need the owner's written approval.
