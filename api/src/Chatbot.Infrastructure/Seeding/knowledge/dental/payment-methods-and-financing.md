We want paying for care to be simple. Below you will find the ways to pay, and how financing works if a larger treatment is more than you want to pay at once.

## Payment methods

We accept cash, debit cards, and credit cards from Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover. HSA and FSA cards are accepted as well. Payment for your share of the bill is due at the end of each visit. We will give you an itemized receipt that you can send to your insurance company.

## Financing with SmilePay Plus

For larger treatments, our financing partner SmilePay Plus offers monthly plans of up to 24 months at 9.9% APR, subject to approval. The application takes about five minutes at the front desk or online. The lender makes the approval decision, not our clinic, so we cannot promise a specific plan before you apply.

## Before you agree to a treatment

Ask for a written estimate before any treatment starts. The estimate shows your share after insurance and helps you compare payment options calmly, without pressure.
