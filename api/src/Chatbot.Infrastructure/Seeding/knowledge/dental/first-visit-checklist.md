Welcome! Your first visit is a chance for us to get to know you and your smile. Here is what to bring and what to expect.

## What to bring

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of the medicines you take, including supplements. If you have recent dental X-rays from another dentist, bring them too, or ask that they be sent to us.

Please arrive 20 minutes early so you can finish the forms without rushing.

## Paperwork

You will fill out a medical history form and a consent form. The medical history helps the dentist see allergies, past surgeries, heart conditions and bleeding problems before any treatment starts. Honest answers keep you safe, and your details stay private as explained in our privacy note.

## During the visit

Your first visit usually begins with a check-up and a conversation with the dentist about your options. We will not start any treatment without your agreement. Bring your questions; no question is too small.
