Your medical record is confidential. We protect your data to the standards the law requires, and we use only what we need to treat you and bill your visits.

## How we protect your data

We comply with HIPAA and limit access to medical records to the team members who need them. We do not ask for medical details through this chat: if they are needed, we discuss them in person or by phone.

## Sharing information

We do not share your data with third parties without your written authorization, unless the law requires it or to process a claim with your insurance. If an insurer asks for information, we will tell you before we send it.

## Your rights

You can request a copy of your medical record. We provide it within 30 days, and you can also ask us to correct an incorrect detail. To do either, write to the front desk or ask for the form at the counter.
