A tooth-colored resin filling repairs a cavity and restores the shape and function of the tooth. Recovery is usually quick and uneventful.

## First few hours

The local anesthetic lasts about 3 hours. During that time, do not chew on the treated side, and be careful with your lips and cheeks, which can be bitten without you noticing. Avoid very hot drinks until normal sensation returns.

## The following days

It is normal to feel some sensitivity to cold or when chewing for a few days. If your bite feels high or uncomfortable, call us: sometimes a small adjustment is all it takes. Avoid chewing ice or very hard foods on the side of the filling.

## Daily care

Brush and floss as usual. A filling does not protect against new decay at its edges, which is why regular check-ups matter, so the dentist can monitor the filling.
