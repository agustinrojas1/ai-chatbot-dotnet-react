We understand that unexpected things come up. Please let us know as early as possible, so we can offer that time to someone else.

## Cancellations without a charge

You can cancel your appointment without a charge if you do so at least 24 hours in advance. You can let us know through WhatsApp or by calling (212) 555-0142 during opening hours.

## Late cancellations and no-shows

If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, a late-cancellation charge of $45 applies. If you miss your appointment and did not let us know, the no-show charge is $65. Both charges are paid at your next visit or at the front desk.

If you had a genuine unexpected situation, please call us anyway. We review each case calmly and, when possible, we can reconsider the charge.

## Rescheduling instead of cancelling

If you only need to change the day or time, it is better to reschedule. The notice rules are the same as for cancellations, so we recommend asking as early as possible.
