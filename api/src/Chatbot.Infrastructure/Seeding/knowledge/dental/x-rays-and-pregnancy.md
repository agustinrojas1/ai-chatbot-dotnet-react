Dental X-rays help detect cavities, gum problems and injuries that cannot be seen with the naked eye. We only take the images the dentist needs.

## Dose safety

Our digital X-rays use up to 80% less radiation than traditional film. Depending on the case, we place a lead apron and a protective collar on you, and the team takes the images from another room.

## Pregnancy and breastfeeding

If you are pregnant or think you might be, let us know before the X-ray. In general, routine X-rays are postponed until after delivery. In an urgent situation, the dentist decides with you which images are necessary.

## Before your first X-ray

We will ask for your medical history and ask about medications and the possibility of pregnancy. Please answer honestly, so the dentist can decide with more confidence.
