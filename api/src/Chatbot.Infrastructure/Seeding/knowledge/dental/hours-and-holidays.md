Our regular hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00, and Saturday, 9:00 to 13:00. We are closed on Sundays.

## Holidays in 2026

We are closed on Monday, May 25 (Memorial Day), Saturday, July 4, Monday, September 7 (Labor Day), Thursday, November 26 (Thanksgiving Day) and Friday, December 25. On December 24 we close at 12:00.

## Schedule changes

When the team has training or the office needs maintenance, we announce it in advance in our chat. After-hours emergencies follow the emergency line procedure, even on a holiday.

If a holiday falls on a weekend, check our website or ask at the front desk before you come in, because hours on those days may change.
