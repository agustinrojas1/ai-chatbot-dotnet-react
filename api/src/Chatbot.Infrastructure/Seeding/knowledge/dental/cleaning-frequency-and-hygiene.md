The ideal frequency depends on your mouth, your habits and your medical history. These are general guidelines: the dentist will tell you what suits your case.

## General guidelines

For most adults, one professional cleaning and check-up a year is the foundation of prevention. Some people who tend to build up plaque, have sensitive gums or have a history of gum disease need cleanings more often, every 3 or 4 months.

## Between visits

Brush twice a day for two minutes with fluoride toothpaste, and use floss or interdental brushes once a day. An alcohol-free mouthwash can help, but it does not replace brushing.

## Signs to call us

Call us if you notice bleeding when brushing that does not improve within a week, red or swollen gums, persistent bad breath, or a tooth that feels loose. Do not wait for your next cleaning to get in touch.
