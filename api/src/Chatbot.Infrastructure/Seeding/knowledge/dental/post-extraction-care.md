Simple extractions heal well when you follow a few steps. Your dentist will also give you written instructions, and those always take priority over this note.

## The first day

Keep the gauze we place in your mouth, biting gently, until the bleeding slows. Do not rinse hard or spit for the first day, and sleep with your head slightly raised. Eat soft, cool foods and avoid hot drinks until the numbness wears off.

## The first week

Avoid using straws for five days, because suction can dislodge the clot. Do not smoke, and keep brushing gently around the area starting the next day. Mild swelling usually peaks around day two and then improves. Take over-the-counter pain relief as directed on the label, unless the dentist advises otherwise.

## When to call us

Call us if bleeding continues after an hour of firm pressure, if you have a fever, or if the area becomes more painful after day three instead of better. For urgent problems outside opening hours, follow the instructions in our emergency information.
