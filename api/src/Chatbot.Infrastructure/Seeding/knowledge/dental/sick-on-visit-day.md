If you wake up with a fever, a cold or another contagious illness, please do not come to the clinic. Call us as soon as you know, so we can protect other patients and our team.

## Calling us

Call the front desk at (212) 555-0142 during opening hours, or send us a WhatsApp message. Tell us your name and your appointment time. We will move your visit to the next available opening, and the front desk will review any late-cancellation charge with you, case by case.

## Fever guideline

Patients with a fever of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher should stay home and reschedule. The same applies to a contagious cold or flu-like symptoms. Please call before your appointment time, not after you have arrived.

## Coming back

You can book a new visit once you have been free of fever for 24 hours without taking fever-reducing medicine. If your symptoms get worse, or you develop severe tooth pain outside opening hours, call the emergency line at (212) 555-0199.
