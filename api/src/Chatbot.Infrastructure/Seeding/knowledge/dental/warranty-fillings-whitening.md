We stand behind our work. This note explains what our warranty covers, what it does not, and how to request help.

## Fillings

Composite fillings placed by our team are covered for two years against failure, such as a crack or a filling that comes loose, as long as you keep your regular check-ups. Wear from grinding and new decay in a different tooth are not covered by the warranty.

## Whitening touch-ups

If the result of your in-office whitening fades, we include one touch-up visit within 90 days at no charge. Staining from coffee, tobacco or new habits after that visit is outside the warranty, but we are glad to discuss options.

## Making a claim

Contact the front desk with the date of your original visit and a short description of the problem. We will book a check-up at the next available time, and the dentist will decide with you whether a repair is needed.
