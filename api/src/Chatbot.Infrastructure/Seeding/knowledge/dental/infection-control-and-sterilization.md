Every patient deserves clean instruments and a clean room. Here is how our team protects you from infection at every visit, from the first check-up to the last.

## Sterilizing instruments

Reusable instruments are cleaned, packed and sterilized in a steam autoclave at 134°C (273°F) after every patient. Each pack is sealed and dated, and it stays closed until it is opened for the next visit. The autoclave is also checked every week with a biological test.

## Single-use items

Needles, saliva ejectors and many suction tips are single-use and thrown away after each patient. The team wears new gloves for every patient and changes masks between visits. Bibs and napkins are also single-use.

## Cleaning the room

The treatment chair, light handles and countertops are covered with disposable barriers, which are changed after every patient. Surfaces are then disinfected with a hospital-grade disinfectant. Each room gets a full clean at the end of the day, and the water lines are flushed for two minutes each morning.
