We want your visit to happen on time, so we send a reminder before every appointment. The reminder comes by WhatsApp, and you can reply to it without calling the front desk.

## When we send reminders

We send a first reminder 48 hours before your appointment and a second reminder two hours before your start time. Both messages show the date, the time and the treatment booked, so you can check the details quickly.

## Replying to a reminder

Reply 1 to confirm your visit, or reply 2 to start rescheduling. If we do not hear from you within 24 hours after the first reminder, a team member will call you at the number on your file.

## Changing a visit after a reminder

Rescheduling needs at least 24 hours' notice to avoid a late-cancellation charge. A change made less than 24 hours before your start time counts as a late cancellation, and the $45 charge applies. If you cannot make it, please tell us as early as you can, so we can offer the time to someone else.
