Severe tooth pain or a broken tooth cannot always wait. These are the options depending on the time and the seriousness of the problem.

## During opening hours

Call the front desk and ask for an Emergency Dental Visit. We try to fit urgent assessments in the same day, and the dentist decides with you on the best next step.

## Outside opening hours

Outside opening hours, call our emergency line at (212) 555-0199. A member of the on-call team will call you back and tell you whether it can wait until the next morning or needs attention that same day.

## When to go to an emergency room

If you have bleeding that does not stop with firm pressure for 15 minutes, difficulty breathing or swallowing, or swelling that spreads toward the neck or eye, go to an emergency room or call 911 immediately. In those cases, do not wait for the emergency line.
