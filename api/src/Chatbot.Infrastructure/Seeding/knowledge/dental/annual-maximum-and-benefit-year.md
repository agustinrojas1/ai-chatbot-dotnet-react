Many dental plans limit how much they will pay in a year. Knowing your annual maximum and when your benefit year starts helps you plan larger treatments without surprises at the front desk.

## What the annual maximum is

The annual maximum is the most your plan pays for covered care in one benefit year. Any amount above it is your responsibility. For example, if your plan has a $1,500 annual maximum and has already paid $1,200 this year, $300 remains for the rest of the year.

## When the year resets

Your benefit year starts on the date your plan sets. For many plans this is January 1, but not all of them. If you are not sure, check with your employer or your plan provider before you schedule a large treatment.

## Checking your remaining balance

Our front desk checks your remaining annual maximum during coverage verification, which takes up to two business days. The amount your plan shows may not include claims that are still being processed, so ask us to confirm the figure before you decide on a treatment.
