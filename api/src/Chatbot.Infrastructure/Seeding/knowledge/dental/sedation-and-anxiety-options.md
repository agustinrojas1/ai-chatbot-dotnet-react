Many people feel nervous about the dentist, and that is completely normal. We go at your pace and explain each step before we start.

## Comfort options

Local anesthesia numbs the area, so you should feel no pain during most treatments. For mild anxiety we can offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas), which wears off quickly. Oral sedation, a pill taken before the visit, is discussed case by case during your consultation with the dentist, because it is a medical decision.

## Your stop signal

You are always in control. If you need a break at any point, raise your left hand and we will pause right away. We agree on this signal together before we start, so you know it will be respected.

## Before your visit

When you book, tell the front desk about any past bad experience so we can prepare. You can ask for a quieter room, and a friend or family member is welcome to wait with you in the reception area. Let us know if a particular noise or smell bothers you.
