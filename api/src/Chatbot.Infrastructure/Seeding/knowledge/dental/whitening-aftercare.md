After whitening, teeth can feel more sensitive for a few days, and the color needs time to settle. These tips help keep the result.

## What to avoid

During the first 72 hours, avoid coffee, tea, red wine, tomato sauce, blueberries and beets. It is also best not to smoke. If you cannot avoid a dark drink, use a straw so the liquid passes further back over the teeth.

## Sensitivity

It is normal to feel mild discomfort to cold or sweet foods for one or two days. A toothpaste for sensitive teeth helps a lot. If the pain is strong or lasts more than a few days, call us so the dentist can check it.

## Keeping the color

Brush twice a day and floss every night. For whitening touch-ups, see our warranty policy.
