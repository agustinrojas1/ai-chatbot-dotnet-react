Items are sometimes left behind after a visit, such as a phone, glasses, a jacket or a bag. Here is how our lost-and-found works at Smile Dental Clinic.

## Where to look

Please check your belongings before you leave the treatment room and the waiting area. If you realize later that you left something behind, call the front desk during opening hours. Give a short description of the item and the date of your visit, and the team will check the treatment rooms.

## How long we keep items

We keep lost items at the front desk for 30 days. After that, unclaimed items are donated to a local charity. Keys, wallets and phones are stored in a sealed box, and each item is logged with the date it was found.

## Picking up your item

Bring a photo ID to collect your item. If you cannot come in, you can ask us to mail it to you, and you pay the postage in advance. Mailing takes about five business days.
