Severe weather or a power outage can force us to close, even during regular hours. We do our best to keep you informed and to move your visit without hassle.

## How we announce a closure

If we decide to close because of weather, we post a notice on WhatsApp and call booked patients by 7:30 a.m. on the day of the closure. Please check your messages before leaving home, especially on stormy mornings.

## Rescheduling after a closure

Visits affected by a weather closure are rescheduled at no charge, and no late-cancellation fee applies. The front desk will offer the next open slot that works for you, and you can choose any day with availability.

## Power outages

If the power goes out during your visit, the team pauses treatment and checks that the room is safe. Any unfinished treatment is booked for the next available slot, and the dentist will explain what to do in the meantime.
