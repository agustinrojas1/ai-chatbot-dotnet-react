We cannot approve prescription refills through the chat. Refill requests are reviewed by the dentist, who decides whether a renewal is appropriate for your case.

## How to request a refill

Call the front desk during opening hours with the medicine name, the strength and the name of the prescriber. Please also tell us which pharmacy you use. The dentist reviews each request within two business days and will call you back if more information is needed.

## What we cannot do

We do not refill controlled medications, such as opioid pain medicines, by phone or chat. Those are only prescribed after an in-person visit. Antibiotics are also prescribed only after an examination, so please book a visit if you need one.

## Keeping your medication list current

Keep your medication list up to date and tell us about every medicine you take, including supplements, at each visit. The first visit checklist asks for this list, and it helps the dentist choose safe options for your treatment.
