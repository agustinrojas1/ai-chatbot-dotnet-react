Booking a visit takes just a minute. You can request a time through WhatsApp or by calling the front desk during opening hours, and we will confirm your slot in writing once it is booked.

## Visit lengths and slots

Standard appointment slots are 30 minutes long. Longer treatments, such as Teeth Whitening, Composite Fillings and Simple Tooth Extractions, take 60 minutes. A 60-minute visit must finish within opening hours. On Saturdays we close at 13:00, so a 60-minute visit must start by 12:00.

## What we need from you

Please give us your full name, a phone number, the reason for your visit and your insurance plan name, if you have one. If you are booking for a child under 12, choose the Children's Check-up, which takes 30 minutes and costs $40. For a first visit, please plan to arrive 20 minutes early to finish the forms.

## Booking ahead

You can book up to 60 days in advance. Popular times fill up first, so booking early gives you the best choice of days. If you need to change your booking later, see our rescheduling information.
