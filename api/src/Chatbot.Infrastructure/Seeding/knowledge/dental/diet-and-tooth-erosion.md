What you eat and drink affects your teeth every day. Some foods and drinks slowly wear down tooth enamel, while others help protect it. These simple habits make a real difference.

## Acidic and sugary drinks

Soda, sports drinks, citrus juice and sweetened coffee drinks can soften enamel, especially when you sip them slowly all day. It is better to drink them with a meal, use a straw and rinse with water afterward. Sugary drinks between meals are a common cause of cavities.

## When to brush

After an acidic food or drink, wait about 30 minutes before brushing. Enamel is softer just after acid exposure, and brushing straight away can wear it down. Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste, and brush twice a day for two minutes.

## Foods and habits that help

Water is the best drink between meals. Cheese, plain yogurt and crunchy vegetables such as carrots help protect enamel and stimulate saliva. Chewing sugar-free gum for about 20 minutes after a meal also helps protect your teeth.
