Smoking affects your gums, your breath and how well your mouth heals. We are happy to talk about quitting at any visit, without judgment.

## Effects on gums and healing

Smoking makes gum disease more likely and makes it progress faster. It also slows healing after a tooth extraction, which is why we ask patients not to smoke during the first week after an extraction. Please also avoid smoking for the first 72 hours after whitening.

## Stains and whitening

Smoking stains enamel and can quickly fade the results of whitening. Our warranty includes one touch-up visit within 90 days of whitening. Staining from tobacco after that visit is outside the warranty, but we are glad to discuss options.

## Support to quit

Ask the dentist at your check-up about quitting support. Free help is available by calling the national quitline at 1-800-QUIT-NOW, and the front desk can tell you more about local support options.
