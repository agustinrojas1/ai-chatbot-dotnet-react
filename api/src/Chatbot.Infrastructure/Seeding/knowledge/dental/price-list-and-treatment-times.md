Here is what our main treatments cost and how long each visit usually takes. Prices are in US dollars and cover the treatment itself. Your share after insurance depends on your plan, so please ask the front desk for a written estimate before any treatment starts.

## Preventive and diagnostic care

A Check-up & Consultation costs $50 and takes 30 minutes. It includes a full oral exam and a conversation with the dentist about your treatment options. A Teeth Cleaning costs $80 and takes 30 minutes; it includes a professional cleaning and polish to remove plaque and tartar. Dental X-Rays cost $60 and take 30 minutes, and a Fluoride Treatment costs $35 for 30 minutes.

## Restorative, surgical and emergency care

A Composite Filling costs $150 and takes 60 minutes. A Simple Tooth Extraction, for a single tooth that is not impacted, costs $180 and takes 60 minutes under local anesthesia. An Emergency Dental Visit costs $90, takes 30 minutes and gives a same-day assessment and pain relief.

## Cosmetic care and children

Teeth Whitening is an in-office treatment that costs $200 and takes 60 minutes. A Children's Check-up costs $40, takes 30 minutes and is for children under 12.
