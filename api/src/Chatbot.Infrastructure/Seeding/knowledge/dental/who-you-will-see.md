Our team works together so that every visit feels organized. Here is who does what during a typical appointment, so you know who to talk to.

## The dentist

The dentist performs your examination, makes the diagnosis and explains your options. Every treatment plan is discussed with the dentist, and no treatment starts without your agreement. The dentist also tells you how often you should come in for check-ups.

## The dental hygienist

The hygienist performs Teeth Cleanings, removing plaque and tartar, and gives practical advice on brushing and flossing. Fluoride treatments are applied by the team with the same care and attention.

## Assistants and the front desk

Dental assistants hand instruments to the dentist, take digital X-rays and keep the treatment room ready. The front desk handles bookings, insurance checks, billing and reminders, and is usually the first person you speak with on the phone or on WhatsApp. If you are not sure who to ask for, the front desk will point you to the right person.
