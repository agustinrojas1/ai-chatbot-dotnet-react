We accept most major dental plans. To find out whether your plan is included, send us the plan name and your member number, and our front desk will verify coverage for you.

## Plans we work with

We work with plans such as Delta Dental PPO, Cigna DHMO, MetLife PPO and Guardian DPO, among others. If your card shows a different abbreviation, such as PPO, HMO or DHMO, please give it exactly as it appears on the card.

## Copays

Each plan sets its own copay. For example, with Cigna DHMO the $20 copay applies to a check-up visit. With Delta Dental PPO, the cost of a preventive cleaning depends on your employer's contract, so we confirm it when we verify.

## How we verify

Our front desk verifies coverage within two business days of receiving your details. This chat cannot confirm coverage, but it can help you choose a time in the meantime. Bring your insurance card to your visit.
