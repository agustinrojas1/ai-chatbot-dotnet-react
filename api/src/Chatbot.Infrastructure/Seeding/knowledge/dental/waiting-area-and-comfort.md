Our waiting area is designed to feel calm, so you can relax before your visit. Here is what you will find when you arrive at Smile Dental Clinic.

## Facilities

Free Wi-Fi is available for patients in the waiting area. Phone chargers are at the front desk, and water and coffee are available at no charge. The waiting area has 12 seats, including several armchairs, and the restroom is next to reception.

## Quiet time

If you prefer a quieter space, ask the front desk for a quieter treatment room when you book. We can also lower the lights and the music on request. Tell us about any sound or smell that bothers you, and we will do our best to adjust.

## Visitors

A friend or family member is welcome to wait with you in the reception area during your visit. Please keep the reception area clear for patients who are checking in, and ask the front desk if you need extra seating for a companion.
