We want every patient to reach the chair comfortably. Here is what to know before you visit.

## Getting in

Our entrance is at street level, with a ramp and an automatic door. The clinic is on the second floor and is reached by an elevator. Wheelchair users can transfer to our reclining chairs, and one restroom is fully wheelchair accessible.

## Parking

The Maple Avenue Garage is one block away from the clinic. The first two hours are free when you get your ticket validated at our front desk. Please keep your ticket with you during the visit.

## Other help

If you need a sign-language interpreter or large-print forms, tell us when you book and we will arrange them with a few days of notice. Assistance animals, such as service dogs, are welcome in every area of the clinic. Ask us if you need help finding a drop-off point near the entrance.
