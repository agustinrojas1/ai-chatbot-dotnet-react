Smile Dental Clinic is a general and family dentistry practice. We care for patients of all ages, from first check-ups to adult treatments, and we aim to make every visit calm and clear.

## What we offer

We provide check-ups, teeth cleanings, fluoride treatments, digital X-rays, composite fillings, simple tooth extractions and in-office teeth whitening. Same-day emergency visits are available when possible. We do not offer orthodontics or dental implants in our office, so we refer those to partner specialists.

## Where and when

Our clinic is on the second floor, reached by elevator, and the Maple Avenue Garage is one block away. We are open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00 and Saturday from 9:00 to 13:00. We are closed on Sundays, so please check our holiday closures before visiting on a public holiday.

## Insurance and payment

We accept most major dental plans, and our front desk verifies your coverage within two business days. You can pay with cash, debit cards, or credit cards from Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover, and we also accept HSA and FSA cards.
