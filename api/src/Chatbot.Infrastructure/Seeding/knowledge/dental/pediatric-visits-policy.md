We love seeing young patients, and we work hard to make visits calm and even fun. Here is how we handle children's appointments.

## When to start

We recommend a first dental visit by their first birthday, or as soon as the first tooth appears. Early visits let us check growth, teach brushing habits and spot small problems before they grow.

## Booking and accompaniment

Children under 12 book the Children's Check-up. Patients 12 and older book the regular Check-up & Consultation. Children must be accompanied by a parent or guardian for the whole visit, and a parent or guardian must sign the consent form before treatment. Teens 12 and older may attend alone once a parent has signed that form in advance.

## Tips for a calm visit

Bring a favorite toy or book, and tell the team about any fear your child has. We explain each step in simple words before we do it. Please avoid saying the visit "won't hurt" and describe it as a clean-up day instead. Sugary drinks between meals are a common cause of cavities, so we will talk about snacks too.
