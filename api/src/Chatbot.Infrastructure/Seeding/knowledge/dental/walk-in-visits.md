We recommend booking every visit so we can reserve the time for you. We do see walk-in patients when we can, but we cannot promise a same-day appointment for routine care.

## Routine walk-ins

If you come in without an appointment, the front desk checks for an open slot. Routine walk-ins are seen only when a chair is free, and the wait can be up to 45 minutes. Booked visits always take priority, so an open slot may not be available at busy times.

## Dental emergencies

A walk-in with severe pain, a broken tooth or swelling is assessed as soon as possible. The front desk may ask a few questions first to decide how urgent the problem is. Outside opening hours, call the emergency line at (212) 555-0199 instead.

## Walk-in prices

Walk-in visits are charged at the same prices as booked visits. For example, a Check-up & Consultation costs $50, a Teeth Cleaning costs $80 and an Emergency Dental Visit costs $90. Payment is due at the end of the visit.
