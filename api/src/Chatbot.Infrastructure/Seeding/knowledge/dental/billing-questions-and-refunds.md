If something on your bill does not look right, tell us. Most billing questions can be solved with one phone call, and our front desk will explain each line clearly.

## Itemized statements

Every bill comes with an itemized statement that lists each treatment, its price and your share after insurance. We reply to billing questions within two business days. If your insurer has already paid part of the bill, the statement shows the amount applied to your account.

## Overpayments and refunds

If you paid more than your share, we refund the difference to the card or account you used, within 14 business days. The front desk issues refunds once the amount has been confirmed against your statement.

## Disputing a charge

To dispute a charge, write to the front desk within 60 days of your visit. Include the date, the treatment and the amount you question. The office manager reviews each dispute and calls you back within five business days with an answer.
