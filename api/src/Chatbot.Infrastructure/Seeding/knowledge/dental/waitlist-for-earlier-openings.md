If your preferred day is full, you can join our waitlist. Openings often come from cancellations, and the waitlist helps us fill them quickly and fairly.

## Joining the waitlist

Joining the waitlist is free. Tell the front desk the treatment you need, the days and times that suit you and the earliest date you could come in. We will add you to the list for that treatment.

## Being offered a slot

When an opening appears, we contact the first matching patient on the waitlist by WhatsApp. You have 30 minutes to reply. If you do not reply in time, we offer the slot to the next person on the list.

## Keeping your place

Your place stays on the waitlist for up to 60 days. Once you accept a slot, the usual rules apply: cancel with at least 24 hours' notice to avoid the $45 late-cancellation charge. A slot you accept from the waitlist counts toward your two free reschedules.
