Plans change, and moving your visit is simple. You can reschedule through WhatsApp or by calling the front desk during opening hours.

## Free rescheduling

You can reschedule up to two times free of charge for each appointment. Please ask at least 24 hours before your current start time. We will offer the next open slot that works for you, and you can choose any day with availability.

## Requests inside 24 hours

Requests made less than 24 hours before the visit count as late cancellations, so our cancellation policy applies. If your situation is urgent, please call us right away so we can talk through your options.

## Same-day changes

Changes on the day of your visit must go through the front desk by phone, so that a team member can check the new time with you. Once the time is agreed, we will send a written summary with the date, time and clinic address.
