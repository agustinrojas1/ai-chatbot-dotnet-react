Some care needs more than one appointment. Here is how we plan longer treatments, so you know what to expect before you commit to anything.

## The treatment plan

After your consultation, the dentist writes a treatment plan that lists each visit, its length and its cost. Most visits are 60 minutes or less. A Composite Filling takes 60 minutes, while a Check-up & Consultation takes 30 minutes, so a plan is easy to fit around your week.

## Estimates and payment

You receive a written estimate before the first visit. The estimate shows your share after insurance for the whole plan. Payment for each visit is due at the end of that visit, and you receive an itemized receipt each time.

## Pausing and changing a plan

You can pause a treatment plan between visits for up to 3 months without losing your place. If you need to move a visit, the usual rescheduling rules apply, so ask at least 24 hours before your start time.
