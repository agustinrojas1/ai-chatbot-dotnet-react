We want every patient to be addressed the way they prefer. You can tell us a preferred name and pronouns at any time, and we will use them for appointments, reminders and in the treatment room.

## Recording your details

The front desk asks about a preferred name and pronouns during booking and at check-in. Your legal name is still needed for insurance claims, billing and records, because insurers match claims to the name on the card. Your preferred name is shown on your appointment reminders and at the desk.

## Updating your record

You can update your preferred name or pronouns at the front desk during any visit, or by writing to us. Changes are saved to your record within one business day, and you will receive a short confirmation.

## Respect in our clinic

If anyone uses the wrong name or pronouns, please tell the front desk or the dentist, and we will correct it straight away. Our team uses the names and pronouns patients give us, and we do not ask for reasons.
