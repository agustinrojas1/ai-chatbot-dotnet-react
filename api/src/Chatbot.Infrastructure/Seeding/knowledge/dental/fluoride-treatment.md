A fluoride treatment is a quick and comfortable way to help strengthen tooth enamel and reduce the risk of cavities. It is one of our preventive services and takes only a short time in the chair. Ask the front desk to add it to your visit.

## What happens during the visit

A Fluoride Treatment costs $35 and takes about 30 minutes. The team applies a topical fluoride gel directly to your teeth. The treatment is painless and does not require anesthesia, so you can leave the clinic right after.

## After the treatment

For 30 minutes after the application, avoid eating or drinking, so the fluoride has time to work on the enamel. Do not rinse your mouth right away, and spit gently. Brushing with fluoride toothpaste at home, as usual, continues the protection between visits.

## Who benefits most

Fluoride is especially useful for children, adults who have had cavities before, and people with dry mouth or receding gums. Some patients need it more often than others, so the dentist will tell you how often to have it at your check-up.
