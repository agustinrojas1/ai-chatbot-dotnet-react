Your feedback helps us improve. Positive comments are always welcome, and so are concerns. We take every complaint seriously and reply in writing when we can.

## How to send feedback

You can share feedback at the front desk, by WhatsApp, or on the feedback card at the counter. Please include the date of your visit and a short description of what happened. Feedback can be anonymous, but a name helps us reply to you.

## What happens next

We acknowledge every complaint within two business days. The office manager reviews it, speaks with the team member involved and sends you a written reply within 14 days with the outcome and any changes we plan to make.

## If you are not satisfied

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can ask for a meeting with the lead dentist within 30 days. You also have the right to contact the licensing authority for dentists in your state, and our front desk can give you its contact details on request.
