Traffic, train delays and parking can slow anyone down. If you are running late, please call the front desk as soon as you know, so we can plan the rest of your visit.

## The 10-minute grace period

If you arrive up to 10 minutes after your start time, we will still see you. The front desk may shorten the visit a little so the schedule stays on time for the next patient, and the dentist will tell you what can be completed that day.

## Arriving more than 15 minutes late

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, the slot may be given to a patient on our waitlist. We will then offer you the next open time, and the front desk can rebook you on the same day if one is free.

## Late arrivals are not cancellations

Arriving late is not a cancellation, so the $45 late-cancellation charge does not apply to it. If you do not come at all and do not call us, the $65 no-show charge applies instead.
